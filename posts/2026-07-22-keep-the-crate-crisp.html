The question I hear most at the Thursday pickup is not about recipes. It is "how do I keep all this alive until next week?" The answer is about ten minutes of work the night you bring the crate home. Do these things before you put your feet up, and next Wednesday's kale will still snap.

Roots: cut the tops tonight

Beets, carrots, radishes and turnips all come with their leaves on, because the leaves are good to eat and they tell you how fresh the root is. But leaves keep pulling water out of the root, so the root goes soft in days. Twist or cut the tops off, leaving an inch of stem, the night you get home.

  • Roots go in a bag in the crisper drawer. Beets keep a month, carrots three weeks.
  • Greens go in a separate bag with a damp towel, and get cooked within three days. Beet greens are chard by another name.
  • Radish tops wilt fastest. Make them into pesto the first night.

Greens: wash, spin, wrap

Lettuce, kale and chard last longest when they go into the fridge clean and nearly dry. Swish them in a sink of cold water, spin them, and roll them loosely in a dish towel inside a bag. Head lettuce from our tunnel keeps a full week like this. Kale keeps two.

If a bunch has gone limp, it is not lost. Cut the ends and stand it in a glass of cold water in the fridge for an hour. It drinks and stands back up.

The fridge is not where vegetables go to wait. It is where they go to rest after you have done your part.

Never the fridge

Tomatoes, garlic, onions, potatoes and winter squash stay out. Tomatoes lose their flavor below 50 degrees. Potatoes turn sweet and gritty. Keep them in a dark, cool spot, away from the onions, which make potatoes sprout.

Herbs like flowers

Basil sits on the counter in a jar of water, like a bouquet, out of the sun. Parsley and cilantro do the same thing in the fridge with a bag over the top.

That is the whole system. Every crate comes with a recipe card, and if a vegetable stumps you, ask whoever is at the pickup table: we have all been there. And if you are not a member yet, the Stand Card lets you try a few crates first.
